礼品卡钱包 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

我们不收集你的任何数据

礼品卡钱包不需要账号，不联网，没有广告、统计或追踪。你录入的礼品卡、卡号、余额、记账记录、PIN 和商家徽标，只保存在你自己的 iPhone 上，不会上传到任何服务器，开发者也看不到。

相机

只在你扫描礼品卡条形码时使用。画面在手机上识别，不保存、不上传。

照片

通过系统的照片选择器，只读取你选中的那一张图片，用来识别条形码或导入商家徽标。app 不会访问你的照片图库。

PIN

礼品卡的 PIN 保存在 iPhone 的钥匙串里，只有这个 app 能读取。

Apple 钱包

加入 Apple 钱包的卡在你的手机上生成。卡进入 Apple 钱包后，由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

删除数据

删除 app 即删除它保存在这台 iPhone 上的全部数据；已加入 Apple 钱包的卡需要在钱包里单独删除。

Gift Card Wallet · Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

We collect no data. The app needs no account, makes no network requests and contains no ads, analytics or tracking. Gift cards, numbers, balances, spending records, PINs and merchant logos are stored only on your iPhone and are never uploaded; the developer cannot see them.

Camera is used only to scan a gift card barcode; frames are processed on device and not stored. Photos are accessed only through the system photo picker, reading just the image you choose. PINs are kept in the iPhone Keychain. Apple Wallet passes are created on your device; once added, Apple Wallet data is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes all of its data on this iPhone.
